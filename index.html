Settings

Community questions are answered by an LLM. For instant in-browser generation or re-generating answers directly from your browser, add an API key for Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, or OpenAI.

Before you paste a key:
  • It is stored in this browser's local storage and sent only to the provider you pick when you click an AI button. Anyone with access to this browser profile can read it.
  • Your key is never included in JSON backups, encrypted sync, URLs, or logs. Core features work without an API key.
  • Calls are billed to your account at the provider's rates. Use a dedicated key with a low spending limit, and clear or revoke it when you are done.
  • Gemini offers a free tier through Google AI Studio; check its current limits before relying on it.
