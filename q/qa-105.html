What is the difference between functional and non-functional testing? Give examples of each.

QA / SDET · Gear 1 (Junior) · Fundamentals · Difficulty: easy · AI-drafted, not yet reviewed by a person

In 30 seconds

Functional testing checks what the system does against requirements: features, inputs, outputs and business rules. Non-functional testing checks how well it does it: performance, security, usability, reliability, compatibility and accessibility. Both are needed, because a feature that works but is slow, insecure or unusable still fails the user.

Explained in depth

Functional testing

Verifies behaviour against requirements: "given this input, the system produces this output."

  • Examples: login with valid and invalid credentials, adding items to a cart, calculating discounts, form validation, API returns the right data and status codes.
  • Types: unit, integration, system, smoke, sanity, regression, and user acceptance testing.
  • Based on specifications, user stories and acceptance criteria.

Non-functional testing

Evaluates quality attributes:

AttributeTesting typeExample check
Speed and capacityPerformance: load, stress, spike, soak95 percent of searches return in under 500 ms with 2,000 concurrent users
Safety from attackSecurity: vulnerability scans, penetration testsNo SQL injection; passwords are hashed; access controls enforced
Ease of useUsabilityNew users complete checkout in under 3 minutes without help
DependabilityReliability and recoveryService recovers within 5 minutes after a node fails; no data loss
Works in many settingsCompatibilityLatest two versions of Chrome, Safari, Firefox; iOS and Android devices
InclusiveAccessibilityMeets WCAG 2.2 AA; works with a screen reader
GrowthScalabilityThroughput grows linearly when adding instances
OperationalMaintainability, installability, observabilityDeployment completes without downtime

Why both matter

Requirements often say only what the system should do, so non-functional needs must be made explicit and measurable ("fast" becomes "p95 under 500 ms"). Non-functional defects are often expensive to fix late because they depend on architecture.

Tips

Plan non-functional testing early, automate it in CI where possible (load tests, security scans, accessibility checks), and monitor in production to confirm real-world behaviour.
