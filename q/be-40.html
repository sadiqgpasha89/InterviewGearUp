How does the Circuit Breaker pattern transition between Closed, Open, and Half-Open states?

Backend engineer · Gear 3 (Senior) · Architecture · Difficulty: medium · AI-drafted, not yet reviewed by a person

In 30 seconds

A circuit breaker watches calls to a dependency. Closed: calls pass through and failures are counted. When failures exceed a threshold it opens: calls fail immediately for a cool-down period instead of waiting on a sick service. After the cool-down it goes half-open and allows a few trial calls; success closes it, failure opens it again.

Explained in depth

          failures > threshold                cool-down elapsed
 CLOSED ---------------------> OPEN ----------------------> HALF-OPEN
   ^                             ^                              |
   |        trial calls succeed  |        a trial call fails    |
   +-----------------------------+------------------------------+

States

  • Closed: normal operation. The breaker tracks failures over a window (a failure rate, such as 50% of at least 20 calls, or consecutive failures) and slow calls.
  • Open: requests are rejected at once or served by a fallback (cached data, default response). This protects the caller's threads and lets the dependency recover instead of piling more load on it.
  • Half-open: after a wait, a limited number of probe requests go through. If they succeed, the breaker closes; if any fail, it reopens and the wait starts again.

Configuration choices

  • What counts as a failure: timeouts, connection errors and 5xx responses, but usually not 4xx client errors.
  • Window size, minimum call volume, failure and slow-call thresholds, wait duration, and number of half-open probes.
  • One breaker per dependency (or per endpoint), so one failing service does not trip calls to healthy ones.

Working with other patterns

Always set timeouts, so failures are detected quickly. Retries need exponential backoff with jitter, and should not hammer an open breaker. Combine with bulkheads to cap concurrency per dependency, and expose breaker state in metrics and alerts. Libraries include Resilience4j, Polly and Envoy's outlier detection.

Likely follow-ups

  • What fallback would you use when the breaker for a recommendations service is open?
  • Why should retries and circuit breakers be coordinated?
  • How do you choose threshold values without causing false trips?
