How do you turn large volumes of SaaS product telemetry into features that signal a customer's financial health or churn risk?

ML / AI engineer · Gear 3 (Senior) · Applied ML · Difficulty: medium · AI-drafted, not yet reviewed by a person

In 30 seconds

Aggregate raw events to the account and date level, then build trend and ratio features over several windows: active users divided by paid seats, 7-day versus 90-day activity, admin and billing-page activity, integrations removed, support tickets per active user. Compute them point in time, normalise by account size and seasonality, and check which ones add lift over billing history alone.

Explained in depth

Pipeline

  • Roll raw events (issues created, pages edited, logins, API calls) up to account x day tables in the warehouse or a streaming job; models do not need event-level data.
  • Compute features as of each scoring date from a feature store so training and serving match and nothing from the future leaks in.

Feature families

FamilyExamples
Utilisationactive users / paid seats; share of seats inactive 30+ days
Trend7-day vs 90-day active users; slope of weekly activity; weeks since peak
Breadthnumber of products, projects or spaces in use; integrations connected or removed
Admin and billingbilling page visits, payment method updates, seat reductions, downgrade or cancel page views
Supportbilling tickets, critical bugs per active user, time to resolution
Peoplekey admin or champion deactivated; owner email domain changes

Make them comparable

  • Use ratios and percent changes so a 10-seat and a 10,000-seat customer are on the same scale.
  • Adjust for seasonality and holidays (week-over-week drops at year end are normal).
  • For very high-cardinality event types, group by product area or learn embeddings of usage patterns, but simple aggregates usually carry most of the signal.

Validate

  • Measure lift over a billing-only baseline; usage features often help most for early warning, billing features near the due date.
  • Check for proxies of sensitive attributes and for features that are downstream of collections activity.

Likely follow-ups

  • Why use ratios rather than raw counts?
  • How would you build these features so training and serving match?
  • Which usage signal would you expect to predict non-renewal earliest?
