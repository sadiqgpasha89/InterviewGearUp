What are your strengths and what are you actively working to improve?

Tech lead / Engineering manager · Gear 1 (Junior) · Behavioral · Difficulty: easy · AI-drafted, not yet reviewed by a person

In 30 seconds

Name two or three real strengths that matter for the role, each backed by a short example, then one genuine area you are working on, with specific steps you are taking and evidence of progress. Avoid disguised strengths such as perfectionism, fake weaknesses, or a weakness that is critical to the job. Be honest, brief and specific.

Explained in depth

What interviewers want

Self-awareness, honesty and a habit of improving. They also want evidence: examples, not adjectives.

Strengths

  • Choose two or three relevant to the role: for example debugging methodically, communicating clearly, learning quickly, reliability.
  • Back each with one short example and its result: "I trace problems from symptoms to cause; last quarter I found a memory leak by comparing heap snapshots, cutting restarts to zero."

Area for improvement

  • Choose a real gap that is not central to the job (for a developer: public speaking, delegating, estimating time), not "I work too hard".
  • Show what you do about it: a course, practice, asking for feedback, a habit, a mentor.
  • Show progress: "I volunteered to present at the team demo each month; my last two were rated clear by my lead."

Example

"My strengths are debugging and clear written communication. For example, my incident notes are used as the team's template. I am working on estimating: I used to under-estimate integration work, so now I list the unknowns, add a buffer and compare estimates with actual time at the end of each sprint. My misses have shrunk from about half to about a quarter."

Tips

  • Keep it under two minutes, and never claim to have no weaknesses.
  • Do not blame others or the company in your answer.
  • Be consistent with your CV and references.

Likely follow-ups

  • What feedback have you received about your weaknesses?
  • How would a former manager describe you?
  • How do you measure whether you are improving?
