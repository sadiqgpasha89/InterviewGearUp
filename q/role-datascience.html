Gear 1: Junior (0 to 2 years)

  • When are the mean, median and mode misleading, and how do you choose which summary statistic to report?
  • What is overfitting and how do you detect it using training and validation performance?
  • Explain precision, recall and F1 score, and give examples of when you would favour each.
  • What is a p-value, and what does it not tell you?
  • How do you handle missing values in a dataset, and how does the missingness mechanism affect your choice?
  • What is the difference between supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement learning, with practical examples of each?
  • Why do we split data into training, validation and test sets, and how do you do it correctly?
  • Write SQL to return the top three earners per department, and explain how window functions such as ROW_NUMBER, RANK and DENSE_RANK differ.
  • Explain the difference between correlation and causation, and what confounders and Simpson's paradox are.
  • What are the assumptions of linear regression and how do you check and fix violations?
  • How does k-fold cross-validation work, and when would you use stratified, grouped or time-series variants?
  • What is feature scaling, which algorithms need it, and what is the difference between standardisation and normalisation?
  • How do you detect and treat outliers, and when should you keep them?
  • Explain the bias-variance trade-off and how model complexity, data size and regularisation affect it.
  • How do you use pandas groupby, merge and pivot operations to answer a business question efficiently and correctly?

Gear 2: Mid-level (2 to 5 years)

  • How do you design an A/B test: choosing metrics, randomisation unit, sample size, power and duration?
  • How should you interpret a confidence interval, and how does it differ from a Bayesian credible interval?
  • Compare L1 (lasso) and L2 (ridge) regularisation, and explain when you would use elastic net.
  • Compare random forests and gradient boosting. When would you choose each for tabular data?
  • How do you handle severely imbalanced classes, such as 0.5 percent fraud, in training and evaluation?
  • When should you use ROC AUC versus precision-recall AUC to evaluate a classifier?
  • How do you approach feature engineering for tabular data, and how do you avoid target leakage?
  • What is the multiple comparisons problem, and how do Bonferroni, Holm and Benjamini-Hochberg corrections work?
  • How do you approach time series forecasting: stationarity, seasonality, model choice and backtesting?
  • What is probability calibration, how do you measure it, and how do you fix a poorly calibrated model?
  • Write SQL to compute monthly cohort retention for users based on their signup month.
  • Compare PCA, t-SNE and UMAP for dimensionality reduction. When do you use each and what are the caveats?
  • How does k-means clustering work, how do you choose k, and when does it fail?
  • What strategies do you use for hyperparameter tuning, and how do you avoid overfitting the validation data?
  • How do you communicate data science results to non-technical stakeholders so they lead to decisions?

Gear 3: Senior (5 to 8 years)

  • How do you estimate a causal effect from observational data using propensity scores?
  • What is difference-in-differences, what assumptions does it need, and how do you validate them?
  • What is CUPED and how else can you reduce variance in A/B tests to detect smaller effects faster?
  • Explain Bayesian A/B testing and multi-armed bandits. When would you use Thompson sampling instead of a classic fixed-horizon test?
  • How does gradient boosting work, and how do XGBoost and LightGBM differ and which parameters matter most?
  • How do you interpret a black-box model with SHAP, permutation importance and partial dependence, and what are the pitfalls?
  • How do you build a recommender system, and how do collaborative filtering, content-based and matrix factorization approaches compare?
  • What is survival analysis and why is it better than plain classification for modelling customer churn or time-to-event outcomes?
  • How would you design an anomaly detection system for metrics or transactions, and how do you evaluate it without many labels?
  • What feature selection methods do you use, and when is feature selection worth doing at all?
  • How do you interpret logistic regression coefficients, and how is the model fitted and diagnosed?
  • How do you do a power analysis and choose the sample size and duration for an experiment?
  • What common problems invalidate an A/B test, such as sample ratio mismatch, novelty effects, and network interference, and how do you detect them?
  • How do you monitor a deployed machine learning model for data drift, concept drift and performance decay, and when do you retrain?
  • How would you build a text classification system, comparing TF-IDF with linear models, pretrained embeddings and fine-tuned transformers?

Gear 4: Lead / Staff (8+ years)

  • What is uplift modeling and how does it differ from a standard propensity or response model for targeting marketing campaigns?
  • Explain instrumental variables and regression discontinuity as ways to identify causal effects without randomisation. What assumptions do they need?
  • When and why would you use mixed-effects or hierarchical Bayesian models instead of pooling all data or fitting separate models per group?
  • How do you evaluate an LLM-powered application, such as a RAG question-answering assistant, before and after launch?
  • How do you assess and mitigate unfairness in a machine learning model used for decisions such as credit or hiring?
  • How do you protect privacy when analysing user data or training models, covering anonymisation, k-anonymity and differential privacy?
  • What is training-serving skew and how do feature stores and consistent pipelines help you avoid it?
  • How do you diagnose and optimise slow SQL queries on very large tables in a data warehouse?
  • How do you design a metrics framework for a product, including a north star metric, input metrics and guardrails, and avoid Goodhart's law?
  • How would you design experiments for a two-sided marketplace such as ride-hailing or delivery where user-level randomisation is biased by interference?
  • Walk through an end-to-end customer churn prediction project from problem definition to deployment and business impact.
  • How would you build a demand forecasting system for thousands of products and stores, including hierarchy, intermittent demand, promotions and inventory decisions?
  • Explain Bayesian inference in practice: priors, posteriors, MCMC sampling and how to check a model.
  • Design a real-time fraud detection system for online payments, covering data, models, latency, labels and the operational loop.
  • When do deep neural networks outperform gradient boosted trees on tabular data, and how do you decide which to use?
