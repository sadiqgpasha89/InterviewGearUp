Explain Diffusion Models: Forward noise process and reverse denoising U-Net score matching.

ML / AI engineer · Gear 4 (Lead / Staff) · Generative · Difficulty: hard · AI-drafted, not yet reviewed by a person

In 30 seconds

A diffusion model gradually adds Gaussian noise to data in a fixed forward process, then trains a network, typically a U-Net, to predict the noise at each step. That prediction equals a scaled score (the gradient of the log density), so training is denoising score matching. To generate, it starts from pure noise and removes it step by step.

Explained in depth

Forward process (fixed)

Over T steps, add noise with a schedule beta_t:

q(x_t | x_{t-1}) = N( sqrt(1 - beta_t) * x_{t-1}, beta_t * I )
x_t = sqrt(abar_t) * x_0 + sqrt(1 - abar_t) * eps,   eps ~ N(0, I),   abar_t = product of (1 - beta_s)

The closed form lets you jump straight to any step t, so training does not simulate the chain. By the last step, x_T is nearly pure Gaussian noise.

Reverse process (learned)

A network eps_theta(x_t, t) takes a noisy input and the time step and predicts the noise that was added. The simple training loss is:

L = E_{x_0, eps, t} || eps - eps_theta( x_t, t ) ||^2

This is denoising score matching: the score of the noised distribution is -eps / sqrt(1 - abar_t), so predicting noise is the same as estimating the direction toward higher probability.

U-Net

An encoder-decoder with skip connections (so fine detail survives), residual blocks, self-attention at lower resolutions, and the time step injected through a sinusoidal embedding. Transformer-based denoisers (DiT) are now common as well.

Sampling

Start with x_T ~ N(0, I) and apply the denoiser repeatedly. DDPM sampling is stochastic and uses around a thousand steps. DDIM and other samplers are deterministic or higher-order and need roughly 20 to 50 steps.

Conditioning and efficiency

  • Text conditioning feeds an embedding into the denoiser through cross-attention. Classifier-free guidance blends conditional and unconditional predictions, eps_uncond + w * (eps_cond - eps_uncond), to trade diversity for prompt fidelity.
  • Latent diffusion (Stable Diffusion) runs the process in the compressed latent space of an autoencoder, which makes training and sampling far cheaper.

Likely follow-ups
