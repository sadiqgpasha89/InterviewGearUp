Gear 1: Junior (0 to 2 years)

  • Tell me about a time you received critical feedback. What did you do with it?
  • How do you handle a disagreement with a teammate during a code review?
  • Tell me about a time you helped a teammate who was struggling.
  • How do you prioritize when you have several tasks due at the same time?
  • Tell me about a mistake you made at work and how you handled it.
  • How do you decide when to ask for help instead of continuing on your own?
  • Describe a time you had to learn a new technology or skill quickly.
  • Tell me about a time you worked effectively as part of a team.
  • Tell me about a time you took initiative without being asked.
  • How do you manage stress and pressure when deadlines are tight?
  • Why do you want to work here, and why this role?
  • Tell me about a time you explained a technical idea to someone who was not technical.
  • Describe a time when the requirements or plan changed late in a project. How did you adapt?
  • Tell me about a time you spotted a quality problem before it reached users.
  • What are your strengths and what are you actively working to improve?

Gear 2: Mid-level (2 to 5 years)

  • How do you structure and conduct effective weekly 1-on-1 meetings with engineers?
  • How do you establish engineering career ladders, levels, and transparent progression expectations?
  • How do you structure a 30-60-90 day onboarding plan to get a new software engineer productive quickly?
  • How do you define engineering quality standards and code review SLAs across a growing engineering team?
  • Compare Agile Scrum and Kanban: How do you choose the right delivery framework for your team?
  • How do you guide a team through project estimation using story points, t-shirt sizing, or probabilistic forecasting?
  • How do you apply the 5 Whys root cause analysis technique without assigning personal blame?
  • How do you foster an innovation culture using hackathons, 20 percent time, and engineering demo days?
  • How do you define team mission charters and write actionable OKRs (Objectives and Key Results)?
  • How do you run effective team retrospectives that produce concrete, measurable action items?
  • What are the key mindset shifts required when transitioning from senior IC to engineering manager?
  • How do you create an environment where junior engineers feel empowered to challenge senior proposals?
  • How do you accurately size sprint capacity while accounting for PTO, on-call duty, and maintenance?
  • How do you measure team sentiment, eNPS, and engagement through regular anonymous pulse surveys?
  • How do you identify and address imposter syndrome among high-potential junior and mid-level engineers?
  • How do you run asynchronous standups and reduce calendar fatigue from excessive recurring meetings?
  • How do you bridge communication gaps between product designers and frontend engineering teams?
  • How do you design an engineering internship program that reliably converts students into full-time hires?
  • How do you define the minimum viable product (MVP) scope when stakeholder requirements are vague?
  • Why are metrics like lines of code (LOC) and velocity flawed proxies for engineering productivity?
  • How do you triage urgent customer escalations and hotfix requests without derailing sprint commitments?
  • How do you build an engineering employer brand and technical blog that attracts top engineering talent?
  • How do you champion web accessibility (a11y) and localization (i18n) as non-negotiable standards?
  • How do you establish sustainable working norms around core hours, response times, and Slack etiquette?

Gear 3: Senior (5 to 8 years)

  • How do you handle an underperforming engineer on your team?
  • How do you balance technical debt against new features?
  • Your manager sets a deadline you think is unrealistic. What do you do?
  • How do you deliver difficult, constructive feedback to an engineer without causing defensiveness?
  • Walk through how you design and manage a fair Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).
  • How do you motivate and retain high-performing senior and staff engineers seeking new challenges?
  • How do you recognize early signs of burnout in an engineering team and intervene effectively?
  • How do you foster psychological safety so engineers feel comfortable taking technical risks?
  • How do you mediate and resolve a bitter technical or architectural conflict between two senior engineers?
  • How do you design a structured, inclusive technical interview loop with calibrated scoring rubrics?
  • What is the difference between mentoring and sponsoring, and how do you sponsor underrepresented engineers?
  • How do you balance paying down technical debt with executive demands for rapid product feature delivery?
  • Explain the Architecture Decision Record (ADR) process: Creation, team review, and versioning.
  • Walk through a Build vs. Buy framework when deciding whether to construct an internal service or purchase SaaS.
  • How do you cultivate an RFC (Request for Comments) culture for collaborative technical proposals?
  • How do you safely deprecate and decommission a critical legacy system without breaking business continuity?
  • How do you manage scope creep and shifting requirements mid-sprint without demoralizing the team?
  • How do you identify the critical path in a complex engineering project and de-risk dependencies?
  • How do you manage cross-team dependencies when an initiative requires alignment across multiple platform teams?
  • How do you translate complex technical trade-offs to non-technical executive stakeholders?
  • What criteria determine a Go/No-Go release decision, and how do you design emergency rollback plans?
  • How do you facilitate a blameless postmortem following a major customer-facing outage?
  • What are the responsibilities of the Incident Commander (IC) during a Sev-1 production incident?
  • How do you design a fair, sustainable on-call rotation that prevents alert fatigue and sleep disruption?
  • How do you track operational health metrics like MTTR, MTTD, change failure rate, and deployment frequency?
  • How do you establish Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 service tiers with clear SLA and SLO commitments?
  • How do you manage and maintain culture across distributed, remote, and asynchronous engineering teams?
  • How do you delegate critical projects to tech leads without micromanaging or abdicating accountability?
  • How do you manage up effectively to keep your Engineering Director or VP aligned and informed?
  • How do you conduct annual engineering headcount planning and forecast budget allocations?
  • How do you evaluate vendor SaaS tools and negotiate enterprise contracts to prevent tool sprawl?
  • How do you eliminate knowledge silos and single points of failure (bus factor) on key services?
  • How do you manage former peers after being promoted to lead your existing team?
  • How do you maintain team focus, morale, and psychological safety following company layoffs or pivots?
  • How do you establish automated code quality gates in CI/CD without creating development friction?
  • How do you build a business case for investing in internal developer platforms and productivity tools?
  • How do you structure collaboration when team members span time zones across Americas, Europe, and Asia?
  • How do you handle a brilliant jerk whose high technical output is paired with toxic team behavior?
  • How do you reconcile product management release pressure with realistic engineering team velocity?
  • How do you navigate high-stress launch crunches while protecting your team from burnout?
  • How do you establish equitable on-call compensation, secondary escalation paths, and paging policies?
  • When and how should you establish production release freezes during critical business periods?
  • How do you align engineering team quarterly goals with broader company revenue and efficiency targets?
  • How do you introduce an Engineering Tech Radar to guide enterprise adoption of new libraries?
  • How do you design a transparent, objective 360-degree peer feedback review process?
  • How do you map and eliminate technical single points of failure across legacy infrastructure?
  • How do you communicate project delays and technical setbacks to executive leadership early?
  • How do you encourage an InnerSource model for cross-team contributions to shared internal libraries?
  • How do you secure a dedicated percentage of sprint capacity (e.g. 20 percent) for ongoing technical debt?
  • How do you support and re-skill engineers during strategic pivots when their tech stack is phased out?
  • How do you build team consensus when two respected senior engineers strongly disagree on an architectural approach?
  • How do you plan and execute simulated disaster recovery drills (GameDays) across production systems?
  • How do you guide a team through a multi-hour cloud provider outage while keeping customers updated?
  • How do you embed security champions within product teams to foster a security-first engineering mindset?
  • How do you manage unrealistic sales commitments made to enterprise clients without engineering review?
  • How do you manage hybrid engineering teams comprising both full-time employees and external contractors?
  • Explain the SPACE framework for measuring developer productivity beyond simple commit counts.
  • How do you manage the deprecation, communication, and sunsetting of public customer-facing APIs?
  • How do you solicit and act on upward feedback from your direct reports to improve your management style?
  • How do engineering Guilds and Chapters maintain consistency across decoupled cross-functional squads?
  • How do you respond when executives mandate an immovable deadline with an uncompromising feature scope?

Gear 4: Lead / Staff (8+ years)

  • How do you set technical direction across multiple teams?
  • Tell me about a time your team missed a deadline.
  • How do you build an interview process and keep the hiring bar high?
  • How do you develop a technical vision and translate it into a multi-year engineering roadmap?
  • Explain Team Topologies: Stream-aligned, Enabling, Complicated-subsystem, and Platform teams.
  • How does Conway's Law influence system architecture, and how do you apply the Inverse Conway Maneuver?
  • What changes when scaling an engineering organization from 10 to 50 to 100+ engineers?
  • How do you guide an engineering team through a company reorganization without losing key talent?
  • How do you lead the organizational change when migrating an engineering group from monolith to microservices?
  • How do you coach and mentor first-time engineering managers stepping into leadership?
  • How do you successfully merge two engineering teams with differing tech stacks and cultures?
  • When should you make the executive decision to cancel or rewrite a failing multi-quarter project?
  • Explain the four Staff+ archetypes: Tech Lead, Enterprise Architect, Solver, and Right Hand.
  • How do you manage tech stack consolidation following a corporate merger or acquisition?
  • How do you prepare an engineering department for its first SOC 2 Type II compliance audit?
  • How would you set key results for a data science team whose work affects both product growth and financial health?
  • A finance executive disagrees with your model's recommendation for a major account based on experience. How do you handle it?
  • How do you mentor junior data scientists to move from notebook models to production-quality work?
  • Your initiative needs work from billing engineering, UX design and finance, and each team has other priorities. How do you get alignment?
  • Leadership wants an LLM-based recommendation system quickly, but the data pipelines it depends on are brittle and undocumented. What do you do?
  • What does a high quality bar mean for data science deliverables at principal level?
