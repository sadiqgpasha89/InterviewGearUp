Compare Gradient Boosted Decision Tree frameworks: XGBoost versus LightGBM versus CatBoost.

ML / AI engineer · Gear 2 (Mid-level) · Tree Models · Difficulty: medium · AI-drafted, not yet reviewed by a person

In 30 seconds

XGBoost, LightGBM and CatBoost are all gradient boosting libraries over decision trees. XGBoost is the mature, robust all-rounder with level-wise tree growth. LightGBM is typically the fastest on large data, using histogram binning, leaf-wise growth and GOSS sampling. CatBoost handles categorical features natively with ordered boosting and symmetric trees, and often needs the least tuning.

Explained in depth

Shared idea

Build trees one after another, each fitting the gradient of the loss (the residual errors) of the ensemble so far, with a learning rate (shrinkage) and regularisation.

Key differences

  • Leaf-wise growth (LightGBM) splits the leaf with the biggest loss reduction, giving lower loss with fewer leaves but a risk of deep, overfit trees on small data; control with num_leaves and min_data_in_leaf.
  • GOSS and EFB in LightGBM speed up training by sampling large-gradient rows and bundling sparse features.
  • Ordered boosting in CatBoost reduces prediction shift and target leakage.

Choosing

Tabular data with many categorical columns: CatBoost. Very large datasets and speed: LightGBM. Safe default with the widest community support and tooling: XGBoost. In practice, try two and tune with early stopping on a validation set.

Likely follow-ups

  • What is leaf-wise versus level-wise tree growth and when does leaf-wise overfit?
  • How does CatBoost avoid target leakage for categorical features?
  • How do you tune learning rate and number of trees together?
