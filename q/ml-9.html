What are the symptoms of overfitting, and what techniques prevent it in machine learning?

ML / AI engineer · Gear 1 (Junior) · Fundamentals · Difficulty: easy · AI-drafted, not yet reviewed by a person

In 30 seconds

Overfitting shows as very low training error but much higher validation or test error, with the gap growing as training continues. Prevent it with more or better data, simpler models, regularisation (L1, L2, dropout), early stopping, cross-validation, data augmentation, feature selection and ensembling.

Explained in depth

Symptoms

  • Training accuracy keeps improving while validation accuracy stalls or worsens.
  • A large gap between training and validation scores.
  • Unstable predictions when training data changes slightly, and implausibly complex fitted functions.

Prevention techniques

  1. More data or data augmentation (flips, crops, noise, synonym replacement).
  2. Simpler models: fewer parameters, shallower trees (max_depth), pruning.
  3. Regularisation:
  • L2 (ridge) shrinks weights, L1 (lasso) drives some to zero (feature selection).
  • Dropout randomly disables units during training.
  • Weight decay in neural networks.
  1. Early stopping: monitor validation loss and stop (restoring the best weights) when it stops improving.
  2. Cross-validation to choose hyperparameters without tuning on the test set.
  3. Feature selection and removing noisy or leaky features.
  4. Ensembling: bagging and random forests reduce variance.
  5. Reduce model capacity or use pre-trained representations with transfer learning.

Process points

  • Keep a held-out test set touched only once at the end.
  • Beware of data leakage (information from the target or the future in features, preprocessing fit on all data), which makes results look better than they are.
  • Compare against a simple baseline; if a complex model barely beats it, prefer the simple one.

Likely follow-ups

  • What is data leakage and how can it look like good generalisation?
