Tell me about a time you worked effectively as part of a team.

Tech lead / Engineering manager · Gear 1 (Junior) · Behavioral · Difficulty: easy · AI-drafted, not yet reviewed by a person

In 30 seconds

Describe a specific project, your role and the team goal, how you communicated, supported others, handled a challenge together, and what the shared result was. Use "we" for team achievements and "I" for your own contribution so the interviewer can see your part. Highlight collaboration behaviours such as sharing knowledge, unblocking teammates and giving credit.

Explained in depth

What they are assessing

Collaboration, communication, reliability and how you contribute beyond your own tasks.

Build the story

  1. Context: the team size, the goal and the challenge (a tight deadline, unclear requirements, a new technology).
  2. Your role: what you owned, and what you did to help others.
  3. Collaboration actions: regular check-ins, clear task breakdown, pair programming, thorough code review, sharing documentation, giving credit publicly, raising risks early.
  4. A challenge handled together: a blocker or disagreement, and how the team resolved it.
  5. Result: delivered outcome with numbers if possible, plus team learning or relationships improved.

Example

"Our four-person team had three weeks to build a reporting feature. I took the data layer and, noticing a teammate was blocked on an unfamiliar API, paired with her for an hour, which got her moving. We agreed on a short daily sync and a shared checklist. We launched on time with no critical bugs, and we kept the checklist for later projects."

Tips

  • Do not pick a story where you did everything alone.
  • Balance "we" and "I".
  • Show behaviours (listening, helping, flexibility), not just a nice outcome.
  • It is fine to mention a difficult teammate, but stay respectful and focus on what you did.

Likely follow-ups

  • What role do you usually take in a team?
  • How did you help a struggling teammate?
  • What made that team work well?
