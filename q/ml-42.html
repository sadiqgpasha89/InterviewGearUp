Explain Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT): How do LoRA low-rank adaptation matrices work mathematically?

ML / AI engineer · Gear 3 (Senior) · LLMs · Difficulty: hard · AI-drafted, not yet reviewed by a person

In 30 seconds

LoRA freezes the pre-trained weight matrix W and learns a low-rank update ΔW = B A, where A is r by k and B is d by r with a small rank r. Only A and B are trained, cutting trainable parameters by orders of magnitude. The adapted layer computes Wx + (alpha / r) B A x, and after training B A can be merged into W with no inference overhead.

Explained in depth

The idea

Fine-tuning updates a weight matrix W of shape (d, k) by some change ΔW. Research on intrinsic dimensionality suggests the useful update has low rank. LoRA parameterises it as a product of two small matrices: ΔW = B A, with B of shape (d, r), A of shape (r, k) and r much smaller than d and k (for example 8 or 16).

Forward pass: h = W x + (alpha / r) * B A x.

  • W is frozen (no gradients or optimiser state for it).
  • A is initialised with small random values and B with zeros, so ΔW = 0 at the start and training begins exactly at the pre-trained model.
  • alpha is a scaling constant that keeps the update magnitude stable when changing r.

Parameter savings

For d = k = 4096 and r = 8: the full matrix has 16.8 million parameters, while LoRA trains 2 * 4096 * 8 = 65,536 (about 0.4 percent). Optimiser memory drops accordingly, so large models fit on much smaller GPUs.

Where to apply and how to choose rank

Usually the attention projections (query and value, often all of q, k, v and o) and sometimes the MLP layers. Higher rank adds capacity but also cost; 8 to 64 covers most tasks. Tune r, alpha and learning rate (LoRA often needs a larger learning rate than full fine-tuning).

Deployment advantages

  • After training, merge: W' = W + (alpha / r) B A. Inference then has no extra latency.
  • Alternatively keep adapters separate and swap them per request or per tenant over one shared base model, and store each adapter in a few megabytes.

Limits

Slightly lower quality than full fine-tuning on some tasks that need large changes, and it does not reduce the memory used by the frozen base weights (see QLoRA for that).

Likely follow-ups

  • Why initialise B to zero and A randomly?
  • Which layers do you apply LoRA to and how do you choose the rank?
  • How can several LoRA adapters share one base model in serving?
