Design a clickstream analytics pipeline.

Data engineer · Gear 3 (Senior) · System design · Not yet reviewed by a person (origin not recorded)

In 30 seconds

Collect events with an SDK into a durable log (Kafka or a managed equivalent), validate against a schema, land raw data in object storage, process in streaming for fresh metrics and in batch for authoritative history, and serve through a warehouse or OLAP store.

Explained in depth

Clarify: event volume (say 50,000 per second), freshness (seconds or hours), consumers (dashboards, ML features, ad-hoc analysis), retention, privacy.

Architecture

Client SDK, then a stateless collector that batches events, then Kafka topics (partitioned by user or session id). From there:

  • A stream processor (Flink or Spark Structured Streaming) for sessionisation, enrichment and near-real-time aggregates, serving a store such as ClickHouse, Druid or Pinot.
  • A sink to object storage as Parquet partitioned by date and hour, feeding batch models (for example dbt) for authoritative tables.

Data quality

Schema registry with Avro or Protobuf, a dead-letter topic for bad events, deduplication by event_id, watermarks for late events, bot filtering.

Privacy

Consent handling, hashing or removing PII, retention limits, and a way to honour deletion requests.

Operations

Replay from Kafka or the raw lake, monitoring of consumer lag, freshness and volume anomalies, and compaction of small files to control cost.

Likely follow-ups

  • How do you handle duplicates and late events?
  • How would you sessionise clicks?
  • How would you honour a GDPR deletion request?

Prerequisite concepts

  • How does Apache Kafka partition topics, and how do consumer groups parallelize message consumption? (Gear 2 · Streaming)
  • Compare Tumbling Windows, Sliding Windows, and Session Windows in real-time stream aggregation. (Gear 2 · Streaming)
  • When is One Big Table (OBT) preferred over normalized dimensional schemas in modern warehouses? (Gear 2 · Data Modeling)
