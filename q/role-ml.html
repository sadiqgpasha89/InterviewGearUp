Gear 1: Junior (0 to 2 years)

  • Explain the bias-variance trade-off and how you deal with overfitting.
  • Explain precision, recall and F1. When do you prefer each?
  • Explain the Bias-Variance tradeoff and how it relates to model underfitting and overfitting.
  • What are the symptoms of overfitting, and what techniques prevent it in machine learning?
  • Explain cross-validation strategies: K-Fold, Stratified K-Fold, and Time-Series Split.
  • Define Precision, Recall, F1-Score, and Specificity with confusion matrix formulas.
  • Compare Mean Squared Error (MSE), Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE), and Mean Absolute Error (MAE).
  • Explain Gradient Descent variants: Batch, Stochastic (SGD), and Mini-Batch Gradient Descent.
  • Compare feature scaling techniques: Min-Max Normalization versus Standard Z-score Standardization.
  • How do you handle missing data: Mean/Median imputation versus KNN versus iterative MICE?
  • How do Decision Trees choose splits using Gini Impurity versus Entropy and Information Gain?
  • What is the difference between supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement learning?
  • Why do we split data into training, validation and test sets, and what is each used for?
  • How does logistic regression work, and why is it used for classification despite the name?
  • What is feature engineering and what are common techniques?

Gear 2: Mid-level (2 to 5 years)

  • What is data leakage? Give examples and how you prevent it.
  • What is RAG, and how does it work? Where does it fail?
  • Compare L1 Lasso regularization and L2 Ridge regularization: Why does L1 produce sparse weights?
  • When is PR-AUC (Precision-Recall AUC) preferred over ROC-AUC for imbalanced datasets?
  • How do advanced optimizers (Momentum, RMSProp, Adam, AdamW) accelerate convergence?
  • What is the danger of Target Encoding, and how do you prevent target leakage during training?
  • Compare class imbalance remedies: Random oversampling/undersampling, SMOTE, and Focal Loss.
  • Compare Bagging and Boosting ensemble paradigms in machine learning.
  • Explain Random Forest internals: Bootstrap aggregation, feature subsampling, and out-of-bag error.
  • Compare Gradient Boosted Decision Tree frameworks: XGBoost versus LightGBM versus CatBoost.
  • Explain the Multilayer Perceptron (MLP) forward pass and matrix multiplication mechanics.
  • Compare activation functions: Sigmoid, Tanh, ReLU, Leaky ReLU, GELU, and Swish.
  • Derive the backpropagation algorithm using the calculus chain rule for neural network weights.
  • What causes vanishing and exploding gradients, and how do Xavier/Glorot and He initialization help?
  • Compare Batch Normalization and Layer Normalization: Why does LayerNorm dominate in NLP?
  • How does Dropout regularize deep neural networks during training, and how does it behave at inference?
  • Explain Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN): Kernels, Strides, Padding, and Feature Maps.
  • How do Residual Connections (ResNet) solve the degradation problem in very deep networks?
  • Compare Recurrent Neural Networks (RNN), LSTMs, and GRUs in managing long-term temporal dependencies.
  • Explain subword tokenization algorithms: Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE), WordPiece, and SentencePiece.
  • Compare Dense Vector Embeddings with Sparse Lexical Search (BM25) for document retrieval.
  • Compare vector distance metrics: Cosine Similarity, Dot Product, and Euclidean Distance.
  • What are optimal document chunking strategies for RAG: Fixed-size, recursive character, and semantic chunking?
  • How does MLflow track experiment parameters, metrics, code versions, and model artifacts?
  • Explain Principal Component Analysis (PCA) dimensionality reduction using covariance matrix eigenvectors.
  • How does K-Means clustering find centroids, and how do you pick K using the Elbow method and Silhouette score?
  • Explain DBSCAN density-based clustering and how it discovers arbitrary-shaped clusters with noise.
  • How does Isolation Forest isolate anomalous points using random binary partitioning trees?
  • Compare Collaborative Filtering (matrix factorization) with Content-Based recommender systems.
  • How do Learning Rate Schedulers (Cosine Annealing with Warmup) stabilize deep learning convergence?
  • Explain Early Stopping and model checkpointing callback logic to avoid over-training.
  • What is Data Leakage in machine learning and how do you ensure preprocessing occurs strictly inside cross-validation folds?
  • Compare Hyperparameter Optimization algorithms: Grid Search, Random Search, and Bayesian Optimization (Optuna).
  • How does ARIMA model autoregression, differencing, and moving averages for time-series forecasting?
  • How do recommender systems resolve the Cold Start problem for brand-new users and items?
  • How do you generate high-fidelity synthetic tabular data using CTGANs and Copulas for training data augmentation?
  • In UAT, business users complain the agent gives slightly different answers to the same policy question. How do you handle it?

Gear 3: Senior (5 to 8 years)

  • How do you evaluate an LLM-powered application?
  • Prompting, RAG or fine-tuning: how do you decide?
  • How does LightGBM use histogram-based algorithms and Exclusive Feature Bundling for speed?
  • Derive the Self-Attention mechanism mathematically: Query, Key, Value matrices and Scaled Dot-Product.
  • Why is the dot-product scaled by $\sqrt{d_k}$ in the scaled dot-product attention formula?
  • Explain Multi-Head Attention and how multiple heads learn diverse representation subspaces.
  • Compare Positional Encodings: Sinusoidal encodings versus Learned embeddings versus RoPE (Rotary Position Embedding).
  • Compare Transformer architectures: Encoder-Only (BERT), Decoder-Only (GPT), and Encoder-Decoder (T5).
  • What are the pre-training objectives for Large Language Models: Next-Token Prediction vs Masked Language Modeling?
  • Explain Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT): How do LoRA low-rank adaptation matrices work mathematically?
  • What is QLoRA and how do 4-bit NormalFloat (NF4) and double quantization enable fine-tuning on consumer GPUs?
  • What is Instruction Tuning and how does dataset formatting (Alpaca/ShareGPT format) steer LLM behavior?
  • Compare LLM quantization approaches: Post-Training Quantization (PTQ) versus Quantization-Aware Training (QAT).
  • What is the KV Cache in autoregressive LLM decoding and why does it create GPU memory bottlenecks?
  • Explain Hierarchical Navigable Small World (HNSW) graph indexing for approximate nearest neighbor search.
  • How does Inverted File Index with Product Quantization (IVF-PQ) compress billion-scale vector indexes?
  • How does Hybrid Search combine dense vector embeddings and BM25 sparse scores using Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF)?
  • What role do Cross-Encoder Re-Ranking models play in refining candidate passages retrieved by bi-encoders?
  • Explain RAG evaluation metrics using the RAGAS framework: Faithfulness, Answer Relevance, and Context Precision.
  • What is a Feature Store (e.g., Feast) and how does it prevent train-serve feature skew?
  • Compare model serving runtimes: Triton Inference Server versus TorchServe versus vLLM.
  • What is Dynamic Batching in model inference servers and how does it balance latency and throughput?
  • Explain the difference between Data Drift and Concept Drift, and what statistical tests (KS-test, PSI) detect them.
  • Compare model rollout strategies: Shadow deployment, Canary deployment, and A/B testing in production.
  • How do SHAP (Shapley Additive Explanations) values provide local and global feature attributions?
  • Explain Two-Tower Neural Network architecture for candidate retrieval in large-scale recommender systems.
  • Explain Mixed Precision Training (FP16/BF16) and how dynamic loss scaling prevents underflow.
  • What causes hallucinations in generative LLMs and what architectural mitigations reduce their frequency?
  • How do Guardrails frameworks (NeMo Guardrails, Llama Guard) sanitize LLM inputs and outputs?
  • How do context window extension methods (RoPE scaling, YaRN) allow models to read 128k+ tokens?
  • How do you fine-tune embedding models using contrastive InfoNCE loss over positive and negative pairs?
  • Compare model compression techniques: Pruning, Knowledge Distillation, and Post-Training Quantization.
  • How does ONNX (Open Neural Network Exchange) provide cross-framework model interoperability?
  • How do you measure and mitigate demographic parity and equalized odds bias in machine learning models?
  • How does the Vision Transformer (ViT) apply standard Transformer encoders to non-overlapping image patches?
  • Explain Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs): Minimax game between Generator and Discriminator.
  • How do you profile GPU kernel bottlenecks in PyTorch using the PyTorch Profiler and TensorBoard?
  • Design an end-to-end real-time credit card fraud detection ML pipeline with sub-100ms P99 latency.
  • How do you automate continuous training (CT) loops triggered by data drift thresholds?
  • What are the trade-offs of deploying LLMs on dedicated GPUs versus serverless inference endpoints?
  • What is cross-attention in Encoder-Decoder models and how does the decoder attend to encoder representations?
  • How does Speculative Decoding accelerate LLM token generation using a small draft model and verification?
  • What techniques do you use when an input is larger than an LLM's context window?
  • For classifying and routing billing dispute tickets with an LLM, when would you use RAG, fine-tuning or both?
  • Defaults are under 2% of accounts. How do you keep a risk model useful instead of predicting 'no default' for everyone?
  • After an economic downturn changes customer payment behaviour, how do you detect and handle drift in your ML and LLM collection pipelines?
  • How do you turn large volumes of SaaS product telemetry into features that signal a customer's financial health or churn risk?
  • How does LangGraph support loops and persistent state, and why does that matter for enterprise agents compared with a linear chain or DAG?
  • In LangGraph, how would you require an underwriter or adjuster to approve high-value or high-risk cases before the agent continues?
  • Using Google's Agent Development Kit, how would you route multi-line insurance questions between specialised sub-agents?
  • What problem does the Model Context Protocol (MCP) solve, and how would you build a secure MCP server in front of a core insurance system?
  • How would you design short-term, episodic and long-term memory for an insurance assistant without overflowing the context window?
  • A customer's session with your coverage-change agent crashes halfway. How do you resume without re-asking questions or repeating expensive tool calls?
  • What telemetry and evaluation do you need to run AI agents in production, and how is it different from testing deterministic code?
  • Your claims triage agent loops calling the OCR tool when a customer uploads a blurry repair estimate. How do you debug and fix it?
  • Why shouldn't an LLM calculate insurance payouts and deductibles directly, and how do you design the system instead?
  • How do you turn a vague business request for an agent into user stories and testable acceptance criteria?

Gear 4: Lead / Staff (8+ years)

  • Design an enterprise AI assistant that answers from company documents and can take actions.
  • Explain Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF): Reward Modeling and PPO optimization.
  • How does Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) eliminate the separate reward model required by PPO?
  • How does PagedAttention (vLLM) allocate KV cache memory non-contiguously like virtual memory in operating systems?
  • Explain FlashAttention: How does hardware-aware tiling compute exact attention without materializing $N \times N$ attention matrices?
  • Compare Distributed Data Parallel (DDP) with Fully Sharded Data Parallel (FSDP) for multi-GPU training.
  • Explain ZeRO (Zero Redundancy Optimizer) memory stages: Partitioning optimizer states, gradients, and parameters.
  • Explain Diffusion Models: Forward noise process and reverse denoising U-Net score matching.
  • Design an enterprise RAG system that searches internal enterprise documents while enforcing ACL permissions.
  • Design a multi-tenant LLM API gateway featuring token budgeting, prompt caching, and semantic routing.
  • Design a large-scale recommendation system for an e-commerce platform, from candidate generation to ranking and serving.
  • How would you design an ML platform for 100 data scientists covering experimentation, training, registry and serving?
  • How would you set up an evaluation and governance programme for LLM applications across an organisation?
  • How do you design a safe, reliable LLM agent system that uses tools, with permissions and human-in-the-loop controls?
  • How do you plan GPU capacity and control costs for model training and inference at scale?
  • How would you design a RAG system over 10 million enterprise documents?
  • How would you design a model that predicts B2B SaaS invoice delinquency before it happens?
  • Build or buy: would you fine-tune an open-weight LLM or use a hosted API for an AI collections outreach assistant?
  • How do you prove the ROI of a data science initiative whose goal is reducing risk rather than growing revenue?
  • Your delinquency model is usable now but you believe more work could improve it significantly. Do you ship or keep iterating?
  • A payment-risk decision must be made in under 200 ms at checkout or retry time. How do you design it?
  • How do you make a credit or collections risk model fair, explainable and compliant?
  • How do you run a valid experiment on billing workflows when related accounts interfere with each other?
  • How would you optimise the length of the grace period before restricting service for an overdue customer?
  • Your default-rate forecast broke during a macroeconomic shock. How do you make forecasting more robust to shocks?
  • Emails, in-app notices, CSM calls and agencies all touch an overdue account. How do you attribute recovered payments to each channel?
  • How would you design communication between a claims intake agent and a fraud detection agent, and how is agent-to-agent communication different from tool calling?
  • How do you enforce privacy, access control and fair-treatment rules in agentic insurance workflows?
  • A motor claim suggests the car was used for ride-hailing, which the policy excludes. Walk through how your agent should process it.
  • A stakeholder wants an agent to auto-approve 80% of health and motor claims within 5 minutes with no human involvement. How do you respond?
