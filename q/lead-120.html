How would you set key results for a data science team whose work affects both product growth and financial health?

Tech lead / Engineering manager · Gear 4 (Lead / Staff) · Planning · Difficulty: medium · AI-drafted, not yet reviewed by a person

In 30 seconds

Write one or two objectives tied to company goals, then pair lagging business results (write-offs, days sales outstanding, retention) with leading indicators the team can move within the quarter (adoption of the tool, cycle time, model precision at capacity). Make each key result measurable with a baseline and owner, include a guardrail so one metric is not gamed at the expense of customers, and review monthly.

Explained in depth

Principles

  • Key results describe outcomes, not tasks: 'ship the risk model' is a milestone; 'reduce 60+ day overdue balance by 10 percent' is a result.
  • Balance lagging results (slow but what the business cares about) with leading ones the team sees move within weeks.
  • Every result needs a baseline, a target, a data source and an owner. If you cannot measure it today, the first key result is building the measurement.
  • Add a guardrail so improving one metric does not damage another (cash collected up, churn of contacted customers flat).

Example

Objective: Make collections proactive and less manual through data and automation.

Key resultType
Cut enterprise write-offs by X% year over year versus a holdoutLagging, financial
Reduce average days to resolve overdue invoices from A to BLeading, operational
70% of the finance operations team use the prioritised worklist weekly within 90 days of launchAdoption
Churn among contacted accounts no higher than the holdoutGuardrail

Research work

  • Exploratory work is uncertain, so use learning key results with a clear decision at the end ('by week 8, decide whether usage telemetry adds at least 5 points of recall; ship or stop').
  • Keep a small share of capacity for platform and data quality work and say so explicitly, rather than hiding it.

Running them

  • Co-write with finance, product and engineering partners so the results are shared, not a data science wish list.
  • Review monthly; a result at 70 percent of an ambitious target can be a good outcome, and changing a key result mid-quarter needs a written reason.

Likely follow-ups

  • How do you write a key result for exploratory research?
  • What guardrail metric would you pair with cash collected?
  • How do you handle a key result that depends on another team?
